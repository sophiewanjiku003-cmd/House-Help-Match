About HouseHelp Match

Our Mission

We aim to bridge the gap between employers seeking quality domestic workers and skilled househelps looking for employment opportunities. Our platform makes it easy, safe, and transparent for both parties to connect and build mutually beneficial relationships.

What We Offer

  • Verified Profiles

    All users undergo verification to ensure genuine and safe connections.

  • Easy Job Posting

    Employers can quickly post jobs and receive applications from interested candidates.

  • Skill Matching

    Our system intelligently matches employers with househelps based on skills and experience.

  • Reliable Support

    Our support team is available to assist with any questions or concerns.

Our Team

HouseHelp Match is built by a dedicated team of professionals committed to improving the domestic worker marketplace. We believe in fairness, transparency, and quality service for all our users.
